Myntra_Sales_Analysis_Power_BI_Report
This Power BI Report Gives Insights about Revenue and Orders over a period of time along with High Performing products insights which helps Stake Holders to make Data Driven Decisions for inventory management and serve there customer in a better way and also sufficient quantity stock for High Demand Products in the shelf to avoid the sales loss

Project Details:

1. Project Overview:
   
This Power BI report was developed to analyse the Sales and financial performance of an Myntra 
e-commerce company which was facing growth and customer satisfaction challenges
The Report provides data-driven insights across:
Sales Performance, Customer Insights, Profits Insights
The objective is to support Stakeholders in making informed decisions to drive sustainable growth

2. Business Objectives:
 
The report addresses the following strategic questions:

 Overall Sales Performance
•	What is the total revenue trend over time?
•	Which products and categories drive maximum sales?
•	Which locations generate the highest sales?
•	Different Segment Sales performance?
•	Sales by Discount Details

 Customer Insights
•	Top 10 Customers?
•	Overall Customers and Unique Customers

Profit Insights
•	Profit by Year and Category
•	Profit by Sate and Category
•	Profit by Ship Mode
•	Profit by Discount Details

3. Dataset Description:
   
The dataset includes Consolidated order-level transactional data with the following key fields
Order Details, Product Details, Customer Details, Region Details and Sales Data
